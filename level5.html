Level 5: Trains Galore and Beyond

Class-1

Safe
Psychologically unsettling
Minimal entities

Description

Level 5 appears to be a massive train station. There are countless platforms, waiting areas, ticket booths, tunnels and maintenance rooms scattered throughout the level. The station seems to continue far beyond what should be physically possible. Some platforms lead to other platforms, some lead into tunnels, and some appear to have no destination at all. There are trains constantly arriving and departing. Some are completely normal. Others have strange designs, incorrect destination signs or doors that lead somewhere they shouldn't. The trains themselves are not always dangerous. Most of them appear to follow some kind of schedule, although nobody has been able to determine what the schedule is. The station is usually quiet between trains. When a train approaches, however, the entire station can become extremely loud. There are also large yellow lines along the edge of every platform. Nobody crosses them. Not even the people who have been here for years. It is an unspoken rule.

Hazards

Screaming Lights

The lights along the platforms are known as Screaming Lights. They look like ordinary station lights at first. However, whenever a train approaches, they begin producing a loud screaming sound. The sound becomes louder as the train gets closer. The lights do not appear to have any speakers or other obvious source of sound. Nobody knows why they scream.

Yellow Lines

Every platform has a yellow line near its edge. Do not cross it. The wind produced by an approaching train can become extremely powerful without warning. Anyone standing beyond the yellow line may be thrown toward the tracks or further into the station. The trains do not need to be moving particularly fast for this to happen. The yellow lines should therefore be treated as a strict boundary. There is no sign explaining this. Everyone simply knows.

Unscheduled Trains

Some trains arrive without appearing on any of the station's boards. Their destinations are often unreadable, missing, or written in symbols that do not appear anywhere else in the level. It is not recommended to board these trains.

Bases

E.G.O.L. Base

The E.G.O.L. base is located inside an old station administration building connected to the main platforms. The base contains sleeping quarters, storage rooms, medical supplies, food and water supplies, a workshop, communication equipment and maps of explored levels. The base is considered one of the safest areas of the level

Objective
