Classifications

Class 0

Safe and stable. Little to no danger is present.

Class 1

Generally safe, with minor hazards that are usually easy to avoid.

Class 2

Moderately dangerous. Hazards or may pose a significant risk.

Class 3

Dangerous. Serious hazards or hostile, requiring caution to survive.

Class 4

Extremely dangerous. Survival is difficult and serious threats are common.

Class 5

Extreme danger. Low chance of survival.

Class Deadzone

Unlivable in. Extremley low chances of survival and hazards are everywhere.

Some levels may have unique classes
