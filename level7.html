Level 7: Mug Land

Class- 0

Safe
No entities
No hazards

Description

Level 7 appears to be a large mug shop. The shop contains shelves, tables and displays filled with mugs. There are many different types of mugs throughout the level, with some appearing completely ordinary while others are strangely shaped or decorated. The walls are covered in framed pictures of mugs. These pictures appear to be mugshots of the mugs themselves. The shop seems to continue far beyond what should be physically possible.

Events

Shaking

At random intervals, the ground and the mugs throughout the shop begin shaking. The shaking can last for several seconds. Some mugs may fall from shelves and displays during the event. The shaking eventually stops, and the level returns to normal.

Objective

Find the purple star-shaped mug and drink from it to escape. Back to Home
