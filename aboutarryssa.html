ABOUT ME

WHO AM I?

My name's Arryssa. If you're reading this, then you've probably already read some of my diary logs. I don't really know why I'm making this page. I guess I should, though. I need to remember who I am and stuff like that. I don't want to forget my own name. That would be really bad.

WHY I WRITE THESE

I started writing these logs so I don't lose my mind. I know that sounds dumb, but I don't think it is. Sometimes I forget things. Small things at first. What day it is. How long I've been here. Where I was going. What I was doing. Then it's bigger things. I don't want to forget who I am. I don't want to wake up one day and not know my own name or where I came from or what I've done. So I write everything down. The levels. The entities. The hazards. The exits. Things I see. Things I hear. Things I find. Stuff that probably doesn't matter too. If I write it down, then I can come back and read it later. If I can read it, then I can remember it. At least that's how it works. I think.

ABOUT ME

My name's Arryssa. Yes, I already said that, but I'm putting it here again in case I forget. I don't know my exact age anymore. Time here is weird, and I stopped trying to count it a while ago. I don't remember how I got here either. I remember being somewhere else. I think. Maybe. I have memories of a place before this, but they're getting harder to remember. Sometimes I remember something, and then a few days later I can't remember what it was. That's why I'm writing this. I like quiet places. I like places where I can sit down and not have to worry about something chasing me. I like writing, even though my spelling is probably horrible. I like having something to do with my hands. I don't like getting lost. I don't like entities. I don't like things that move when I'm not looking at them. I don't like things that make sounds behind me. I don't like places where the walls change. I don't like pink. I REALLY don't like pink. Level 2 was horrible.

THINGS I KNOW ABOUT MYSELF

My name is Arryssa. I can write. I can remember some things. I like quiet. I hate pink. I don't like being alone, but I also don't always like being around people. I carry my stuff with me. I write things down when I'm scared. I write things down when I'm not scared too. I don't know why I do that. I think writing makes me feel like I'm still me.

WHY I KEEP GOING

I don't really know. Maybe I just wanna get out. Maybe I wanna find someone. Maybe I just don't wanna forget everything. Maybe those are all the same thing. I think the biggest reason is that I don't want to disappear without anyone knowing I was here. If I stop writing, then maybe I'll forget. If I forget, then what has even left of me? I don't know if that's how it works, but I'm not taking the chance.

IF I FORGET

If you're reading this and I don't remember writing it, then hi. My name is Arryssa. You're reading my diary. I write these so I can remember things. I don't know where I am. I don't know how I got here. I don't know how long I've been here. But my name is Arryssa. Remember that. Please tell me who I am if I forget. Please tell me what I've done. Please tell me what I was doing. And please don't lie to me. I think I'd rather know the truth, even if it's bad.

ONE LAST THING

If I ever write something here that says I'm not Arryssa, don't believe it. Unless I wrote this part wrong. Or unless something happened and I forgot. Wait. No. Don't think about that. My name is Arryssa. I'm Arryssa. I know that. I think I know that. I need to know that.
