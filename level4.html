Level 4: The Open Field

Class-3

Dangerous
Environmental hazards
Minimal entities

Description

Level 4 is a massive outdoor field with no visible buildings, roads or structures. The grass is short and pale green and the sky is always cloudy. There is no rain and there is no visible sun. There is also no visible horizon. The field seems to continue forever. Sometimes there are trees scattered around the field. Some are completely normal while others have no leaves at all. There are also old wooden fences that do not seem to surround anything. Sometimes you can see things very far away. Buildings, trees, fences and sometimes even people. Walking towards them does not seem to make them any closer. Eventually they disappear. The field is not completely empty. It just looks like it is.

Hazards

The Wind

There is almost always wind in Level 4. Most of the time it is weak and harmless. Sometimes it suddenly stops. If this happens, do not move. Travelers who continued walking while the wind was completely still have reported hearing footsteps behind them. Nobody has been able to identify where the footsteps come from.

The Sky

The sky never seems to change. The clouds can remain in the same position for hours. Sometimes something can be seen moving between them. Nobody has been able to identify what it is. Do not stare at the sky for too long.

Dark Grass

Most of the grass is safe to walk through. There are sometimes patches that are noticeably darker than the grass around them. Avoid these patches. They have been reported to move when nobody is looking directly at them.

Entities

Spindels

Objective

Find the road. There is only one road in Level 4. It does not appear until you are relatively close to it. Once you find it, follow it. Do not leave the road. Do not follow voices coming from the field. Do not follow people. Especially if they look like you.
