LOG 1

I don't know when I started keeping these records. That is probably the first thing I should write down. There are no clocks here. At least, none that I have found that actually work. I tried counting the hours by hunger, then by sleep, then by how often the lights flicker. None of them gave me the same answer twice. So I stopped trying. The first room was white. That is the easiest part to remember. White walls. White—actually, no carpet. White lights. No windows. No doors. Just the same room repeating in every direction, with the buzzing overhead following me wherever I went. I thought it was a building at first. Then I realized buildings are supposed to have an outside. I have not found one. I started marking the walls with whatever I could find. Scratches, pieces of paper, my pen. Anything that might prove I had already been somewhere. Some of the marks disappeared. Some of them appeared somewhere else. I have decided not to investigate that. Instead, I am going to document everything I find. Levels. Entities. Strange objects. Anything that might help someone else understand this place. If someone finds these records, then hopefully they will know one thing before anything else: Do not assume that an empty room is actually empty.

LOG 2

LEVEL 0: A BEGINNING

I have decided to call this place Level 0. I don't know if that is its actual name. It is simply the first level I found, and calling it something makes it easier to write about. Level 0 is a maze. Everything is white. The walls are padded. The floor is padded. There are no rooms, no windows, and no obvious landmarks. Just corridors that seem to continue forever. The first thing I noticed was the floor. I couldn't trust it. Sometimes it felt soft. Sometimes it felt wet, even though it wasn't. Once, it felt rough enough that I thought I had somehow stepped onto concrete. Then I realized something worse. The floor can drop. I don't know why. I don't know where it leads. I only know that touching the floor doesn't tell you what the floor actually is. Touch cannot be trusted here. Neither can sight. After staying here for a while, I started noticing something in my vision. Static. At first, it was barely there. Small patches that disappeared when I blinked. By the second day, there was more. By the third, it was becoming difficult to see through it. I don't know how long it takes before the static completely takes over someone's vision. I don't intend to find out. Then there are the screams. They sound like people. Sometimes they sound like they are coming from somewhere behind me. I have heard them more than once. I turned around the first time. I won't be doing that again. There is also the problem of direction. After spending enough time walking through the maze, I stopped knowing which way I had come from. I couldn't tell whether I was going forward or walking in circles. I started leaving marks on the walls. I stopped trusting those too. There are entities here. I don't know what they're called, but I called them these: Wranglers and Spindels. I don't know much about either yet. I am going to keep my distance until I do. I wanna escape. That should be easy. It isnt.

LOG 3

LEVEL 1: THE DROWNED HALLS

Just escaped Level 0. Level 1 is strange. Not bad strange, just... different. After Level 0, I was expecting another maze. Another endless hallway with nothing in it except the lights and whatever else is hiding there. Instead, I found a hotel. At least, I think it's a hotel. There are halls, rooms, balconies, staircases, beds, couches, kitchens, bathrooms, vending machines, and a bunch of other stuff that shouldn't be here. Most of it is underwater. Not completely underwater, though. Usually, the water only reaches my ankles or knees. It's completely clear too, which is honestly kinda weird. I tried drinking it. It's safe. I don't know why. I don't think I want to know why either. Everything here is lit by this soft blue light. I can't find where it's coming from. There aren't any normal lights that I can see, but somehow the whole level is still lit. There are windows too. Wish there weren't. They don't show the outside. They show the ocean. An ocean that doesn't seem to end. I've seen shapes moving out there. Large ones. Like REALLY large. I don't know what they are, and I'm not going to go find out lol. The water rises and falls throughout the level. Most of the time, it isn't a problem. Sometimes a hallway that was ankle-deep becomes knee-deep. Sometimes more. I found one section that was completely flooded. Nope. Didn't go in. There is one thing I noticed about the water, though. When it suddenly becomes completely still, leave. I don't know why. I don't know what happens. And I'm not going to find out. There are entities here too. I call 'em Drowners. I've only seen them from a distance so far. Hopefully, it stays that way. The strange thing about Level 1 is that there doesn't seem to be an objective. No maze to escape. No obvious exit. No reason to leave if you don't want to. There are even beds here. I could probably stay here for a while. That might be the strangest part. A place where I can actually stop walking. I don't think I'll stay forever. But for now... I think I'll rest. Hopefully, that's not a stupid idea.

LOG 4

LEVEL 2: PINK REVERIE

Just got out of Level 1. I think I'm starting to get used to this place, which is probably not a good thing. Level 2 is pink. Like REALLY pink. Everything is pink and bright, and it's honestly kind of awful to look at for too long. There are neon pink lights everywhere. They're fluorescent, and some of them are HUGE. I mean billboard huge. I learned pretty quickly that staring at them isn't a good idea. My eyes started hurting after looking at one for too long, so yeah... don't do that. Some of the lights are loose too. I heard one crash somewhere behind me. I'm not going back to check what happened. The lights are also heavy. REALLY heavy. If one of those giant ones falls on you, you're probably not getting back up. The wind here is also insane. I don't understand how there can be this much wind indoors, but somehow there is. It just starts randomly too. One second everything is normal, and then suddenly the wind is pushing me backwards and throwing stuff everywhere. I almost lost my bag because of it. I don't know where the wind comes from. There doesn't seem to be any vents or anything that could cause it. There are entities here too. Shapeshifters, Tripwire Goblins, and these pink rabbits. The rabbits are weird. They look completely normal except they're pink. That's literally it. At first I thought they were just normal rabbits that somehow got pink, but then I noticed some of them move in really specific ways. One rabbit kept walking the same route. Turn, stop, walk, turn, stop. Over and over again. So I followed it. I copied everything it did. Every turn. Every pause. Everything. And eventually I found a pink door. I thought I was done. It wasn't open. Apparently, you have to follow the rabbit's entire route correctly for the door to unlock. I guess I missed something the first time because it just stayed there. I followed the rabbit again, and this time I copied every movement as closely as I could. The door opened. So that's the objective here. Follow the rabbit. Copy its movements. Find the pink door. Hope you didn't mess up. You can apparently walk past the exit without even realizing it's the exit if you haven't followed the right route. Honestly, that's kind of cruel. There are probably other ways to escape, but I'm not going to test that. I don't like this level. Too pink. Too windy. Too many things that can fall on my head. But at least the rabbits helped. I think.

LOG 5

LEVEL 3: THE OVERGROWN ATRIUM

Level 3 is probably the nicest place I've seen so far. Which is probably exactly why I don't trust it. It's basically a giant shopping mall, but in the middle is this huge greenhouse. There's a massive glass dome above everything, and the whole place is covered in moss and ferns. The moss is almost completely white. It looks dead, but it's still growing somehow. The sunlight is weird too. It looks like late afternoon, but it never moves. I stayed in one spot for what felt like hours, and the shadows didn't change at all. It's warm here. The air smells like wet soil, and everything feels kind of damp. There are old wooden kiosks everywhere, most of them are rotting away. There's fountains too, but they're all cracked and filled with this weird mist. The weirdest part is the silence. No wind. No birds. No bugs. Nothing. Well. I thought there was no wind. There actually is wind here. A LOT of it. It just doesn't make any sound. It can go from barely moving the leaves to suddenly being strong enough to throw plants around. One second it's like standing in front of a fan, and the next it's basically a hurricane. I nearly got knocked over once, and I didn't even hear the wind coming. I don't know where it comes from. The fountain in the middle seems important. I don't know why yet, but things start getting weird if you walk too far away from it. The escalators move. Not like they start moving normally. They change where they are. I went down one hallway, and when I looked back, the escalator wasn't there anymore. There was a completely different storefront instead. I walked around trying to find it again and somehow ended up in a part of the mall I had already been through. Except it wasn't the same anymore. The stores were different. The hallways were different. I couldn't find the fountain. I started panicking a little. Eventually, I found another moss-covered wall and walked through it. It didn't work. So I tried another one. That one worked. I ended up back near the fountain. There are multiple walls covered in moss and other creeper plants throughout the level. Apparently, only one of them is actually an exit. I don't know how you're supposed to figure out which one. I also saw two things I already know. Wranglers. Tripwire Goblins. I wasn't expecting to see them here. I thought they only stayed in the levels where I found them before. Maybe they don't. Maybe none of the entities actually stay in one place. That's a thought I don't really want to think about right now. For now, I'm staying near the fountain. It's quiet here. Too quiet, maybe. But after everything else... I think I can deal with quiet.

LOG 6

LEVEL 4: THE OPEN FIELD

Just got out of Level 3. I think. Honestly, I don't even know how I got here. One second I was walking through that stupid greenhouse, and then suddenly there's grass everywhere. Actual grass. I thought I finally got outside for a second, but there's no way this is normal. Level 4 is just a giant field. That's it. No buildings. No roads. No houses. Just grass and trees and these weird fences that don't even seem to surround anything. The sky is cloudy all the time. No sun. No rain. Just clouds. I tried looking for a horizon, but I couldn't find one. The field just keeps going and going and going. There are things in the distance too. Sometimes trees. Sometimes buildings. Sometimes people. I tried walking towards one of the buildings because I thought maybe it was an exit, but it never got any closer. I kept walking, and it just got farther away until it disappeared. The wind here is weird. It's always blowing, and it's usually not that strong, but sometimes it just stops. Completely. No wind at all. That happened earlier. I stopped walking when it happened because I remembered what happened in the last level when I ignored something that felt wrong. Then I heard footsteps behind me. I didn't turn around. They stopped after a while. I waited until the wind came back, and then I kept walking. I'm not checking what made those footsteps. There are Spindels here too. Of course there are. I saw one standing in the field really far away. I looked away for maybe a second, and when I looked back, it was closer. I looked away again, and it was even closer. So now I'm trying not to look at it and also trying not to look away from it, which is GREAT. I eventually just kept walking. Then I saw someone standing near the road. I thought it was another person at first. I waved at them. They waved back. Then I realized they were wearing the exact same clothes as me. I didn't stop walking. They didn't follow me. At least, I don't think they did. I found the road after that. It wasn't there before. I swear it wasn't. It just appeared behind me. I'm following it now. I really hope this is the exit. I don't want to find out what happens if I get lost in this field.
