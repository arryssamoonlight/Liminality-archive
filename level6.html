Description

Level 6 appears to be a classic American neighborhood. There are houses, roads, sidewalks, lawns, driveways, trees and other features commonly found in suburban neighborhoods. The neighborhood seems to continue far beyond what should be physically possible.

Hazards

Extreme Heat

Temperatures in Level 6 can rise to around 50°C. The heat is dangerous to humans and can cause heat stroke if someone remains outside for too long. The heat can also kill trees.

Extreme Cold

Temperatures in Level 6 can fall to around -50°C. The cold is dangerous to humans and can cause severe freezing conditions throughout the neighborhood. Trees and houses can be seen shivering during extreme cold. Some smaller trees and houses may die if the cold lasts long enough.

Snow and Hailstorms

Snow and hailstorms can occur during periods of extreme cold. Snow can accumulate to at least 10 feet in severe storms, covering roads, cars, lawns and parts of houses. The storms can make traveling through the neighborhood extremely difficult.

Entities

Events

Food Drops

Food occasionally falls from the sky throughout Level 6. The food appears to primarily feed the houses and trees, although it is completely safe for humans to eat. Food can include anything from carrot cake and pizza to fruit, bread, sandwiches and other ordinary foods. During a Food Drop, mushrooms also begin growing in areas of exposed dirt. The mushrooms are completely safe for humans to eat. The amount of food can vary greatly. Small food drops may only cover a small area, while larger food drops can cover entire streets. The houses and trees often appear unusually harmless while feeding during this event. This behavior can be used to lure humans and other entities closer, so approaching entities during a Food Drop is not recommended.
