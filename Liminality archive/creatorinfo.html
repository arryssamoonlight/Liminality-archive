ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Liminality Archive is a fictional database documenting strange levels, entities, hazards and other things found throughout the archive.
Most of the information is written like it is part of an actual database, even though none of it is real.
The diary logs are written differently because they are supposed to be personal records rather than proper archive entries.

WHY DID I MAKE THIS?

I like the idea of websites that look like they contain information they probably shouldnt.
I wanted this to feel less like a normal story and more like something you could actually stumble across while looking through the internet.
So I made an archive.
Then I made some levels.
Then I made some entities.
Then somehow I made minigames.
So here we are.
