Level 2: Pink Reverie

Class-4

Dangerous entities
Environmental hazards

Description

Level 2, known as Pink Reverie, is a large pink-themed level containing numerous environmental hazards and entities. Despite its colorful appearance, the level is extremely dangerous and travelers should remain cautious while exploring it. The level contains several hazards that can make navigation difficult, including extremely powerful winds and large fluorescent lights.

Environmental Hazards

Neon Pink Fluorescent Lights

Neon pink fluorescent lights are present throughout Level 2. Staring directly at them for too long can cause blindness. Some of the lights are loose, and extremely large billboard-sized lights can fall from above. Due to their enormous size and weight, being struck by one can be fatal.

Super Heavy Winds

Despite being indoors, extremely powerful winds can suddenly occur throughout Level 2. These winds can make movement extremely difficult and may cause loose objects to be thrown around. The winds can appear without warning, and their source is currently unknown.

Entities

Shapeshifters
Tripwire Goblins

Pink Rabbits

Entity Class-0
Native to level 2 pink rabbits appear to be completely ordinary rabbits just pink and show no unusual physical characteristics. However, some rabbits may occasionally follow unusually precise routes through the level. Travelers who reproduce a rabbit's movements exactly, including its turns, pauses, and changes in direction, may discover clues related to escaping Level 3. It is unknown whether the rabbits intentionally guide travelers or whether their movements are simply coincidental.

Objective

Escape. How? Eventually, a rabbit route may lead a traveler to a pink door. This door is the exit from Level 2, but reaching the door does not necessarily allow a traveler to leave. The traveler must have correctly reproduced the complete movement pattern of the rabbit they followed. If even part of the pattern was missed or performed incorrectly, the door will remain locked. Travelers may therefore pass the exit multiple times without realizing that they have already found it. Once the complete rabbit pattern has been correctly followed, the pink door will open and allow the traveler to leave Level 2.
