Entities

Wranglers

Entity Class:

Class 1

They look roughly like stick figures made out of string, with excess string crumbled around their faces. They attack travelers by grabbing and restraining them, attempting to strangle them and violently contort their limbs. Despite that, they're classified as Class 1 entities because they're relatively easy to defeat. Their main weakness is the string around their face. Pulling or grabbing that string can kill them. Once a Wrangler is dead, its body can serve as a food source.

Spindels

Entity Class:

Class 2

Spindels are horrifying spider-like creatures with animal-like arms that are definitely not spider legs. They will attempt to grab travelers and throw them, but their grip strength is relatively weak. They can also bite travelers, and their bites release poison into the victim's body. This poisonous bite is the main reason Spindels are classified as Class 2.

Drowners

Entity Class:

Class 0

Small, pale creatures that resemble people wearing soaked clothing, but their faces are always hidden beneath wet hair. They don't normally attack travelers. They simply stand in the water and stare toward the nearest window. If approached, they slowly walk away. If a traveler follows them for too long, however, they will eventually lead the traveler into a deeper section of the level and disappear.

Shapeshifters

Entity Class:

Class 2

Shapeshifters can take the form of something a traveler desires most. Their appearances are often connected to the pink environment of the level and may resemble someone or something emotionally important to the traveler. When approached, the Shapeshifter eventually reveals its true form: a slender black body with a pink heart-shaped head. It attempts to restrain travelers in a prolonged embrace. If the traveler cannot escape, the restraint eventually becomes fatal. However, if a traveler manages to free their hands and strikes the entity once or twice, it will disappear.

Tripwire Goblins

Entity Class:

Class 4

Tripwire Goblins are extremely thin entities that resemble living tripwires. Their thin bodies make them extremely difficult to notice. If a traveler trips over one, it releases a toxic gas that can be rapidly fatal
