Level 3: The Overgrown Atrium

Class-1

Safe
Psychologically unsettling
Minimal entities

Description

The Atrium looks like an abandoned shopping mall that somehow became a greenhouse. There are rotted wooden kiosks, cracked indoor fountains and abandoned storefronts scattered throughout the level The entire level is lit by a warm late-afternoon sunlight. It never moves and it never fades. There is no obvious source for the light besides the giant glass dome above.The air is warm and heavy and smells like wet soil. There is no sound of wind. No animals either. Nothing. Also there are very little entities, very rare to find

Hazards

Distance

The central fountain seems to be the safest place in the level. Walking too far away from it causes the escalators behind you to change position. At first the changes are small. An escalator that was going down might suddenly be going somewhere else. If you keep walking, the changes get worse. Storefronts appear in places they werent before and hallways stop leading where they should. Eventually you can end up trapped in a maze of abandoned stores without being able to find the fountain again.

Silent Varying Winds

Even though you are inside there are still quite a lot of wind. Though it doesn't make sound. Source is unidentifiable. But the speeds vary like crazy, one moment theres barely any wind, the next its a hurricane, the next its like your in front of a fan. They may blow you away or blow plants at you

Entities

Wranglers
Tripwire Goblins

Objective

Stay near the central fountain and find a way out. Find a wall of moss or any creeper plant and walk through there are multiple, only one works
