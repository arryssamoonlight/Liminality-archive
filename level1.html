Level 1: The Drowned Halls

Class-1

Low entities
Water hazard

Description

Level 1 is a massive network of flooded halls, corridors, balconies, staircases, and rooms resembling an abandoned seaside hotel. Most areas are covered in clear water ranging from ankle height to knee height, although some sections are completely submerged. The level is illuminated by a soft blue light with no identifiable source. Large windows can be found throughout the level, but instead of showing the outside, they look directly into a seemingly endless ocean. Large silhouettes can occasionally be seen moving beyond the glass. Despite its abandoned appearance, many of the level's facilities remain functional. Beds, couches, kitchens, bathrooms, vending machines, and other ordinary amenities can be found throughout the level.

Hazards

The Tide

The water level throughout Level 1 slowly rises and falls. Most changes are harmless, but certain areas can become completely flooded. The water itself is safe to touch and drink. However, travelers are advised to avoid areas where the water suddenly becomes completely still.

Entities

Drowners

Objective

There is no required objective in Level 1. Travelers may remain in the level for as long as they wish, provided they remain in safe areas.
