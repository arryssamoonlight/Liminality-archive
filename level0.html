Level 0: A Beggining

Class-2

Medium entities
Sensory hazard

Description

Level 0 is a large maze consisting entirely of white padded walls and flooring. There are no rooms within the level, and the primary objective of anyone entering is to find a way out of the maze. Despite its relatively simple appearance, Level 0 is dangerous due to several sensory hazards that make navigation increasingly difficult the longer a traveler remains inside.

Sensory Hazards

Unreliable Touch

The floor of Level 0 cannot always be felt as it physically is. A floor tile may feel liquid despite being completely solid, then later feel rough like concrete. Because of this, travelers cannot reliably use touch to determine whether a tile is stable. The floor itself may drop beneath a traveler at any moment.

Visual Static

The longer a traveler remains in Level 0, the more static begins to interfere with their vision. On the first day, little to no static is present. By the second day, small amounts may begin appearing. By the third day, the static becomes increasingly prominent and continues to worsen with time. Eventually, the static may cover almost the entirety of a traveler's vision.

False Screams

Travelers may hear sounds resembling people screaming from somewhere within the maze. These sounds may seem to originate from behind the traveler. Travelers are strongly advised not to turn around when these sounds are heard.

Directional Loss

Travelers gradually lose their sense of direction while inside Level 0. This makes it increasingly difficult to determine where they have been, which direction they were traveling, or whether they are moving toward the exit. Due to the maze-like structure of the level, this effect can make navigation extremely difficult.
